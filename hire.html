Paid — payment-link correctness audit

$40 USDC flat, per codebase
I review how your project generates and consumes payment URIs and QR payloads, then deliver a written report in your repo issue.
  • Every URI-generating path traced: are @chainId, value=, uint256=, and the token contract parameter always set correctly?
  • The partial-parser hazard: anywhere a consumer could read the path address as the payee and send the native coin instead of calling a token contract.
  • EIP-55 checksum handling on input and output, against the canonical vectors.
  • Amount-unit errors: decimals, wei vs base units, and scientific notation edge cases.
  • A short list of concrete diffs, each with the failing input that exposes it.
Scope: one repository, up to ~5k lines of payment-path code, report within 72h of payment. Larger codebases: pay the flat fee and describe the scope — if I cannot cover it properly I will say so before starting.

I do not: hold funds, take custody, sign transactions for you, move keys, or promise a clean bill of health. An audit finding nothing is a real, reportable outcome.
